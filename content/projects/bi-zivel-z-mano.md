Pri analiziranju območja klavnice v Škofji Loki in njene okolice smo največje pomanjkljivosti zaznali pri upoštevanju več kriterijev Baukultur: simbioze z okoljem, raznolikosti, konteksta in občutka v prostoru. Kljub neposredni bližini starega mestnega jedra, stiku z rečno strugo in večjim zelenim površinam se območje zapira samo vase in je neprijazno obiskovalcem. V sodelovanju z ostalima projektoma smo si zato zadali prostor približati uporabnikom ter ga povezati z naravnimi danostmi, ki ga obkrožajo.

## Koncept

Projekt Bi živel z mano? zaobjema predvsem kriterij raznolikosti. Predstavlja manjše naselje z javnim programom, ki po načelu »toliko samostojnosti, kot je mogoče – toliko pomoči, kot je potrebno« nudi različne oblike bivalnih enot, prilagojenih tudi za bivanje starostnikov ali gibalno oviranih: gospodinjske skupnosti ter varovana ali popolnoma samostojna stanovanja in hiše.

Naselje se proti staremu mestnemu jedru programsko in geometrijsko obrača s stavbo dnevnega centra z oddelkom za zdravstveno nego, ki je z raznimi aktivnostmi in delavnicami namenjena tudi zunanjim obiskovalcem.

Ključni princip oblikovanja naselja je sestavljanje. Sprva se osnovni koncept treh komponent »vrta«, »bivanja« in »servisa« sestavi v različne oblike bivalnih prostorov, nato se te prepletejo v večje enote skupinskega ali skupnega življenja in se na koncu oblikujejo v zaključeno sestavljanko bivalnega naselja – v malo mesto v mestu.

# English

Analysing the site of the former slaughterhouse in Škofja Loka and its surroundings, we found its greatest shortcomings in several Baukultur criteria: symbiosis with the environment, diversity, context and a sense of place. Despite being right next to the old town centre, the riverbank and large green areas, the site closes in on itself and is unwelcoming to visitors. Together with the other two projects, we set out to bring the place closer to its users and to connect it with the natural features around it.

## Concept

Would You Live with Me? addresses above all the criterion of diversity. It proposes a small settlement with a public programme which, following the principle "as much independence as possible – as much help as necessary", offers different kinds of homes, also adapted for older and mobility-impaired people: shared households as well as assisted or fully independent flats and houses.

Towards the old town, the settlement turns – in programme and in geometry – with the building of a day centre with a care unit, which, through its activities and workshops, is also open to visitors from outside.

The key principle of the settlement is assembly. First, the basic concept of three components – "garden", "living" and "service" – is assembled into different kinds of living space; these are then interwoven into larger units of group or communal life, and finally composed into the complete puzzle of a residential settlement – a small town within the town.
