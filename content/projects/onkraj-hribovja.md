Idejna zasnova prenove hiše v Železnikih, ki je bila že v zelo slabem stanju, v družinsko hišo s turističnimi apartmaji.

## Koncept

Naloga je bila v eno hišo umestiti dva programa – dom družine in apartmaje za goste – in ju preplesti tako, da se med seboj ne motita.

Ključ je bil v legi hiše. Ker je delno vkopana v hrib, ima vhoda na dveh nivojih že vzpostavljena: v hišo se vstopa tako v pritličju kot v 1. nadstropju. Zasnova to izkoristi – v 1. nadstropju je zdaj turistični program s svojim vhodom, družina pa v hišo vstopa skozi pritličje. Vsak program ima tako svoj dostop, svoje poti in svojo zasebnost, hiša pa ostaja ena celota.

# English

A conceptual design for renovating a house in Železniki, already in very poor condition, into a family home with holiday apartments.

## Concept

The task was to fit two programmes into one house – the family's home and apartments for guests – and to interweave them so that neither disturbs the other.

The key was the house's position. Because it is partly dug into the hill, it already has entrances on two levels: one on the ground floor and one on the first floor. The design makes use of this – the first floor now holds the holiday apartments with their own entrance, while the family enters through the ground floor. Each programme has its own access, its own routes and its own privacy, and the house remains one whole.
