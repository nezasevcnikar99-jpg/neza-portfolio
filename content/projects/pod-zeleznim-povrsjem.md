Projekt prenove območja železniškega muzeja obravnava specifičen kontekst industrijske dediščine, ki danes kljub bogatemu vsebinskemu gradivu deluje v omejenem prostorskem okviru. Obstoječe industrijske hale in rotonda predstavljajo dragocen arhitekturni spomin, a zaradi razpršene zasnove in prostorske stiske ne omogočajo zadovoljive muzejske izkušnje.

## Koncept

Projekt se zato zavestno izogne agresivni nadgradnji obstoječih objektov in predlaga drugačno pot: subtilno, a izrazito prostorsko gesto – novo podzemno povezavo, ki združuje vse obstoječe stavbne strukture v enoten, funkcionalno in konceptualno premišljen muzejski sistem.

Nova podzemna struktura je razdeljena v dve etaži. Zgornja etaža je neposredno povezana z muzejsko vsebino: nudi dodatne razstavne prostore, hkrati pa omogoča neprekinjeno pot med posameznimi objekti kompleksa. Spodnja etaža je namenjena razširjenemu javnemu programu, ki prostor odpira širši kulturni in strokovni javnosti: knjižnica, galerija, večnamenska dvorana in konferenčne sobe.

Čeprav sta etaži fizično ločeni, sta zasnovani tako, da obiskovalcu muzejskega in javnega programa omogočata vsaj bežen vpogled v dogajanje na »nasprotni« strani. Med njima se tako ustvarja vizualna povezanost, obenem pa večja kontinuiteta celotnega prostora.

Pomemben prostorski element so tudi tri teatralna stopnišča, pravokotno umeščena na obstoječe hale, ki vodijo v nižje etaže javnega programa. Ta skupaj z nasproti ležečimi ozelenjenimi atriji ne služijo le kot dostopi, temveč tudi kot svetlobniki in prostorski poudarki, ki rahljajo mejo med zunanjim in notranjim, med starim in novim. V podzemlje prinašajo naravno svetlobo, atmosfero in orientacijo, obenem pa ustvarjajo odprt dialog z obstoječo arhitekturo na površju.

# English

The renovation of the Railway Museum site deals with the particular context of industrial heritage which, despite its rich collection, works today within a limited spatial frame. The existing industrial halls and the roundhouse are a valuable architectural memory, but their scattered layout and lack of space do not allow for a satisfying museum experience.

## Concept

The project therefore deliberately avoids aggressive additions to the existing buildings and proposes another way: a subtle but distinctly spatial gesture – a new underground link that joins all the existing structures into a single museum system, considered both in function and in concept.

The new underground structure is divided into two levels. The upper level is directly linked to the museum's content: it offers additional exhibition space and an uninterrupted route between the buildings of the complex. The lower level holds an extended public programme that opens the place to a wider cultural and professional public: a library, a gallery, a multi-purpose hall and conference rooms.

Although the two levels are physically separate, they are designed so that visitors to the museum and to the public programme can catch at least a glimpse of what is happening on the "other" side. This creates a visual connection between them and a greater continuity of the whole space.

Another important spatial element are three theatre-like staircases, set at right angles to the existing halls, leading down to the lower levels of the public programme. Together with the planted atria opposite them, they serve not only as entrances but also as light wells and spatial accents that loosen the boundary between outside and inside, between old and new. They bring daylight, atmosphere and orientation underground, and open a dialogue with the existing architecture above.
