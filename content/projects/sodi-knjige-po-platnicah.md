Zbirka naslovnic za strokovne in poljudne knjige – od vodnikov po tujini do priročnika o varstvu osebnih podatkov in pravnega leksikona. Za vsako knjigo je nastalo več različic, ki isto temo preizkusijo z drugim motivom, barvo ali postavitvijo naslova.

# English

A collection of covers for professional and popular books – from travel guides to a handbook on personal data protection and a legal lexicon. For each book, several versions were made, trying out the same theme with a different motif, colour or placement of the title.
