Grad Hrastovec je imel v prostoru in družbi vedno izstopajočo vlogo. Vpet v lokalni kontekst je hkrati segal prek svojega merila. Slikovit in mogočen stavbni ansambel na vzpetini, nekdaj sedež fevdalnega gospo(dar)stva, kulturno središče, prizorišče sodnih procesov, je danes domovanje velike skupnosti socialnovarstvenega zavoda. Proces deinstitucionalizacije vodi slednjega iz gradu v primernejše prostore, grad pa postavlja pred izziv nove rabe, ustrezne njegovemu izročilu.

## Koncept

Projektni predlog prepozna potencial zunanjih in notranjih prostorov na gradu in v bližnji okolici kot privlačnih prizorišč za dogodke različnih zvrsti in meril, odmevnih v širšem prostoru.

Prizorišča med seboj poveže z obnovljenimi obstoječimi in novimi potmi ter dopolni s podpornimi programi, ki v vsakodnevni rabi razširijo ponudbo dejavnosti na lokalnem nivoju.

Preoblikovano osrednje dvorišče vzdolž zgodovinske vstopne osi učinkovito poveže niz zunanjih grajskih prostorov in jih naveže na nove prireditvene prostore, ki se v podzemlju vzpostavijo med ostanki nekdanjih obrambnih zidov.

Program je razporejen po nivoju zasebnosti. Najbolj javni prostori – prireditveni prostori s kavarno, razstavni prostori in obrtne delavnice – so v kletni etaži, kjer je dostop do njih najlažji. V pritličju, povezanem z osrednjim dvoriščem, so knjižnica, prodajalne, hostel ter prostori za mlade in skupnost, nadstropje pa naseljujejo programi, ki zahtevajo največ zasebnosti: stanovanja, konferenčni prostori ter prostori za rehabilitacijo in rekreacijo. V južnem traktu je program stalen, v severnem pa spremenljiv in se prilagaja potrebam dogodkov v gradu.

# English

Hrastovec Castle has always held a prominent place in its landscape and its society. Rooted in its local context, it has at the same time reached beyond its own scale. A picturesque and imposing group of buildings on a hill – once the seat of a feudal estate, a cultural centre and a place of trials – it is today home to a large community of a social care institution. Deinstitutionalisation is moving the institution out of the castle into more suitable premises, leaving the castle with the challenge of a new use worthy of its heritage.

## Concept

The proposal recognises the potential of the castle's outdoor and indoor spaces, and of its immediate surroundings, as attractive venues for events of different kinds and scales, with a reach beyond the region.

It links the venues with restored existing paths and new ones, and complements them with supporting programmes that, in everyday use, widen the range of activities on offer locally.

The reshaped main courtyard, along the historic entrance axis, ties together the sequence of the castle's outdoor spaces and connects them to new event spaces created underground, among the remains of the former defensive walls.

The programme is arranged by degree of privacy. The most public spaces – event spaces with a café, exhibition spaces and craft workshops – are in the basement, where they are easiest to reach. The ground floor, connected to the main courtyard, holds a library, shops, a hostel and spaces for young people and the community, while the upper floor is home to the programmes that need the most privacy: flats, conference rooms and spaces for rehabilitation and recreation. The programme in the south wing is permanent; in the north wing it is flexible and adapts to the needs of events at the castle.
