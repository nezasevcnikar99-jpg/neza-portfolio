Idejna zasnova prenove zapuščenega objekta v Planini v stanovanjsko hišo z apartmaji. Hiša je zelo specifična: z različnimi nivoji in višinami ter netipično, delno ukopano zasnovo.

## Koncept

Osrednje vprašanje projekta je bilo, kako v eni hiši ločiti dva programa – stanovanjsko hišo in apartmaje – tako, da se njune poti med uporabo ne križajo.

Drugo izhodišče je bilo ohraniti obokane stropove. Zasnova zato posega v obstoječo strukturo s čim manj rušitvami in nove prostore prilagodi različnim nivojem in višinam hiše, namesto da bi jih izravnala.

# English

A conceptual design for turning an abandoned building in Planina into a house with holiday apartments. The house is very particular: it has different levels and heights and an unusual, partly sunken layout.

## Concept

The central question of the project was how to separate two programmes in one house – the family home and the apartments – so that their routes never cross in use.

The second starting point was to keep the vaulted ceilings. The design therefore intervenes in the existing structure with as little demolition as possible and fits the new rooms to the house's different levels and heights instead of levelling them.
