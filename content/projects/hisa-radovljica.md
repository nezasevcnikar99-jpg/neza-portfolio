Idejna zasnova prenove stare hiše v mestnem jedru Radovljice v enodružinsko hišo.

## Koncept

Hiša stoji v strnjenem mestnem jedru, kjer se stavbe stikajo druga z drugo in ob njih ni prostora za vrt ali teraso. Zunanji prostor, ki ga ima družinska hiša navadno ob sebi, je zato v zasnovi prestavljen navzgor – v zgornjo etažo.

Terasa je oblikovana tako, da čim bolj sledi osnovnemu gabaritu hiše. Hiša tako navzven ohrani obliko, ki jo povezuje z okoliškimi hišami mestnega jedra, in ne spremeni podobe ulice, družini pa ponudi zunanji prostor, ki ga pritličje ne more.

# English

A conceptual design for renovating an old house in the town centre of Radovljica into a single-family house.

## Concept

The house stands in a dense historic centre, where buildings touch one another and there is no room beside them for a garden or a terrace. The outdoor space that a family house usually has next to it is therefore moved upwards, to the top floor.

The terrace is shaped to follow the basic volume of the house as closely as possible. From the outside, the house keeps the form that ties it to the neighbouring houses of the old town and leaves the streetscape unchanged, while the family gains an outdoor space the ground floor could never give them.
